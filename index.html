How research scholarships work

  1. Find a supervisor first. Most PhD scholarships need written support from a principal supervisor at the university.
  2. Apply for admission and the scholarship together. At ECU, ANU, Monash and UWA you are considered for scholarships through the research-degree application itself.
  3. Watch the round dates. International rounds usually close months before domestic rounds.
  4. Coursework scholarships are often automatic. ECU's 2027 international tuition reductions need no separate application; you are assessed when you apply for the course.

About this guide

StudyAustralia Grants gathers publicly available scholarship information from official university and government websites so prospective students can compare options in one place. Amounts are in Australian dollars. Status labels update automatically from each listing's published dates. If you spot an error or a scholarship we've missed, please let us know so we can correct it.

StudyAustralia Grants is an independent information service. University names are used only to identify where each scholarship is offered; we are not endorsed by or affiliated with any institution listed.

Information is provided as a guide only and may change. Always confirm eligibility, values and deadlines on the official page before applying.
